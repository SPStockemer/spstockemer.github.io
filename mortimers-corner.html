A Word from Mortimer

Well, hello there!

I'm Mortimer Quill, but you can call me Mort. Everybody does.

Welcome to my little corner of the website! The Boss finally gave me a place of my own. I've been asking for one for ages, although he claims I never mentioned it. That's his story.

I spend most of my time writing, exploring interesting places, and occasionally getting into situations that probably seemed like better ideas beforehand. I particularly enjoy old cemeteries, moonlit walks, and anything that makes ordinary people look over their shoulders.

S.P. writes the scary stories. I prefer to live them. Well, sort of. Being a skeleton does have its advantages.

Anyway, make yourself comfortable, have a look around, and visit whenever you like. There's always something happening around here.

And if the Boss asks, tell him I've been working.

Mortimer Quill

A Few Things About Me

Since you're here, I suppose I should tell you a little more about myself.

My birthday is August 10. I'm not entirely sure how old I am, but I look pretty good for someone who's been reduced to bones.

I enjoy writing, reading, exploring old cemeteries, and taking long walks after dark. The Boss and I have been on a few adventures together, although he insists on bringing a camera everywhere. Personally, I think some things are better left unexplained.

I have a particular fondness for Halloween, but I don't see why we should limit spooky things to one month of the year. That's just poor planning.

People sometimes ask whether being a skeleton makes everyday life difficult. Not particularly. I don't have to worry about getting older, I never need a haircut, and I've saved a fortune on groceries.

Of course, finding clothes that fit can be a nightmare.

But enough about me. There are places to explore, stories to tell, and a whole world full of interesting things waiting in the dark.
