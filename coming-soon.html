What waits beyond the next page

The Haunted Hearth

Coming January 2027

The firelight only reaches so far.

For generations, people have gathered around the fire to share stories of ghosts, old legends, and things best left undisturbed. Some are warnings. Others are family secrets, passed down through the years, growing darker with every telling.

In The Haunted Hearth, ten tales of winter horror explore the fears that survive long after those who first whispered them are gone. From restless spirits to forgotten traditions and memories that refuse to fade, these stories recall a time when the nights were long, the world beyond the window was dark, and the safest place to be was beside the fire.

But the firelight only reaches so far.

The Storybook Lied

Coming April 2027

The happy endings came later.

Once upon a time, fairy tales were not meant to entertain children. They were warnings, passed from generation to generation, preserving truths too terrible to forget.

Over the centuries, those warnings were rewritten. Monsters became familiar characters. Villains were given convenient names. The darker truths disappeared beneath magical wishes and happily-ever-afters.

But the original accounts survived.

In The Storybook Lied, ten tales uncover the forgotten truths behind beloved fairy tales. Drawn from hidden records, old journals, and whispered traditions, these are not the stories generations of children grew up hearing.

These are the stories that came before.

And there was a reason they were changed.
